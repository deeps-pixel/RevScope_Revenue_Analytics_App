Reduce Blanket Discounting

Finding: >20% discounts cause $1.61M erosion without volume gain, dropping margin to 21.13%.

Action: Cap blanket discounting at 15% and shift towards targeted, volume-based discounts.

Optimize Underperforming SKUs

Finding: 58 SKUs (68% of catalog) yield only 20.59% of revenue with low margins (e.g. Classic Jump Rope).

Reassess Channel Operations

Finding: Dealer channel and BR05/BR06 are consistently below average on revenue and margin.

Action: Reassess dealer commissions and audit operating costs at underperforming branches.

Mitigate Seasonal Revenue Dip

Finding: Revenue forecast predicts a seasonal dip heading into June 2026 (drop to $642k).

Action: Plan and execute preemptive marketing campaigns and promotions in late April/May.

Accelerate Overdue Collections
